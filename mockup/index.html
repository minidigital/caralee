Transparent pricing

Fees & billing

We are a mixed billing practice. Eligible patients receive bulk billing.

Bulk billed for

  • Children 15 and under
  • Pension & Health Care card holders
  • Commonwealth Seniors Health Card
  • DVA card holders
  • Health assessments & care plans

Standard consultations

Standard (≤20 min)$80
Long (20–40 min)$120
Extra long (40+ min)$160
Phone appointment$80

Medicare rebates apply. Out-of-pocket typically $35–36.

Important notes

  • $30 fee for cancellations within 3 hours or non-attendance
  • Implanon & IUD procedures bulk billed for eligible Medicare holders
  • Private fees (e.g. driver medicals) payable on the day

Practice information

Policies

Important information about appointments, privacy, and your care at Caralee Medical Group.

Appointments & cancellations

Please arrive on time for your appointment. A $30 fee applies for cancellations within 3 hours of your booked time, or for non-attendance.

Bulk billing eligibility

Bulk billing is available for children 15 and under, pension and health care card holders, Commonwealth Seniors Health Card holders, and DVA card holders.

Privacy

Your personal health information is handled in accordance with Australian privacy legislation and medical practice standards.
